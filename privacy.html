Privacy policy

Kitchen Sync: To the Minute, by The Spectrum Tech Engine. Last updated 9 October 2026.

The short version

Kitchen Sync works out when to put each part of your meal on so it's all ready together. You don't need an account. The only thing shared with other people is the cooking information for products, so everyone who scans that pack gets it.

What is shared with other users

When you add cooking instructions for a product, we save the product's barcode, name, brand and cooking methods (temperatures, times and steps) to a shared database. A random ID that isn't linked to you is saved with it, so wrong entries can be dealt with. Nothing else about you is shared.

Photos of packs

If you photograph the cooking instructions, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI (through Google Firebase) only to read the instructions. Photos are not saved by Kitchen Sync. Google may use what is sent to its free AI service to improve its products, so only photograph the pack, not people or personal items.

What stays on your phone

Your list, your settings (oven type, air fryer, microwave power), the method you prefer for each product, and copies of products you've looked up are kept on your phone.

Product names

When a barcode isn't in our database, we look up its name on Open Food Facts, a free open food database. Only the barcode is sent.

Alarms and camera

The camera is only used to scan barcodes and take photos of instructions you choose to share. Cooking alarms are set on your phone and never leave it.

Anonymous usage counts

To help improve the app, it counts a few anonymous things, such as how many meals are planned and how many products are added. These counts can't be traced back to you and don't include what you cook.

Your choices

You can clear your choices in Settings → Forget my choices on this phone, or uninstall the app. To have a product you added removed from the shared database, or for any privacy question, email privacy@thespectrumtechengine.com.

Food safety

Cooking times come from the pack, or are typical times clearly marked as estimates. Always check food is piping hot all the way through before eating.
